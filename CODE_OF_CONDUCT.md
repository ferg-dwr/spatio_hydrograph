# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. The spatio-hydrograph project is dedicated to creating an inclusive environment where everyone feels valued and respected, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

We pledge that participation in this project and our community will be harassment-free for everyone.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** - Use welcoming and inclusive language
- **Being patient** - Remember that people have different knowledge levels and learning styles
- **Being constructive** - Give and receive feedback graciously
- **Being collaborative** - Work together to solve problems
- **Being professional** - Focus on what is best for the community
- **Assuming good intent** - Give others the benefit of the doubt
- **Celebrating diversity** - Value different perspectives and backgrounds

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuro(a)typicality, physical appearance, body size, age, race, or religion
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate misgendering or use of "dead" or rejected names
- Patterns of unwanted social contact (following/stalking)
- Physical violence or threats of violence
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission ("doxxing")
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above behavior

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to:
- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful
- Explain the reasoning behind moderation decisions when appropriate

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:
- The GitHub repository and related issues/pull requests
- Project discussions and documentation
- Community conversations and meetings
- Social media associated with the project
- Any space where someone is representing the project or community

---

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it to the project maintainers. You can:

### Direct Contact
Contact the maintainers directly as listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)

### Email
Send a detailed report to the maintainer email address (see MAINTAINERS.md)

### GitHub Issue
Open a confidential issue on GitHub (mark it as private if possible)

**Reports should include:**
- Your contact information (name, email)
- Names/usernames of those involved
- Detailed description of the incident
- Context and timeline
- Any supporting evidence
- How the behavior affected you

---

## Investigation Process

When a report is received:

1. **Acknowledgment** - We will acknowledge receipt within 24 hours
2. **Investigation** - We will investigate the complaint privately and thoroughly
3. **Communication** - We will keep you informed of progress
4. **Action** - We will take appropriate action if conduct violations are confirmed
5. **Appeal** - If you disagree with the outcome, you may appeal to senior maintainers

---

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- **First offense**: Warning and request for apology/clarification
- **Repeated offense**: Temporary suspension from project activities
- **Severe offense**: Permanent removal from project and associated spaces

The specific consequence will be determined by the severity of the violation and context.
